# 🗺️ ROADMAP

## Near-Term (0–2 months)
- Server-authoritative `user_created` analytics event at auth boundary
- Harden signup funnel attribution and conversion reporting
- Expand E2E analytics smoke coverage for production parity checks

## Mid-Term (2–6 months)
- Cohort retention and funnel decomposition dashboards
- KPI anomaly detection and alerting
- Experimentation/A-B framework tied to metric registry

## Long-Term (6+ months)
- Multi-tenant analytics segmentation
- Advanced recommendation quality feedback loops
- Deeper operational intelligence for content and growth workflows

## Success Criteria
- High-confidence executive metrics with low reconciliation variance
- Faster learner journey from discovery to apply
- Stable deploy-to-observe cycle with minimal analytics regressions
